About this demo. Larkspur Bakehouse is a fictional bakery and every number here comes from synthetic data built to behave like real sales: weekly and yearly seasonality, US holidays with closures, planned promotions, a store that opened in March 2025, and a 12-day point-of-sale outage at Riverside in July 2025 that is left as missing data, not filled in. The model is gradient boosting on calendar, promo and lagged-sales features, with every lag at least 28 days old, scored by a 6-fold rolling backtest. Built by Ha Le.
